Privacy Policy

One More Move · Sharp App tech · Last updated 3 September 2026

One More Move has no accounts, no login and no servers of its own. We do not run a backend, and the game sends us nothing. Your progress lives on your device and nowhere else. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google's advertising and billing services collect, described below.

Who we are

One More Move is published by Sharp App tech. For anything in this policy, write to sharpapptech@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The game saves the following locally, using your device's standard app storage. None of it is transmitted anywhere, and all of it is erased when you uninstall the app:

  • Level progress, stars and best move counts
  • Coin balance, owned skins and the skin you have equipped
  • Daily reward streak and daily challenge results
  • Sound, music, haptics and notification preferences
  • The time you last opened the app, used only to schedule reminder notifications
  • Counters that pace how often ads appear
  • Whether you have purchased Remove Ads

There is no cloud save and no account, so this data cannot be recovered if you uninstall the app or switch phones. Your purchases are the exception — those are restored by the store, not by us.

Analytics

The published app transmits no analytics. The game contains an analytics interface used during development, and in release builds it is wired to a sink that discards every event. No usage data reaches us or any third party through it.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To select and measure ads, Google collects information from your device. This is Google's processing, not ours — we never receive it. It typically includes:

  • Your device's advertising identifier
  • Device model, operating system version and language
  • IP address, from which approximate location can be inferred
  • Whether you saw, tapped or closed an ad

Google's handling of that data is governed by Google's Privacy & Terms.

Your choices about ads

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Remove ads entirely: the in-app Remove Ads purchase switches off banner and interstitial ads permanently. Rewarded ads remain available only if you choose to watch one.

Rewarded ads are always optional. Nothing in the game is gated behind watching one, and declining costs you nothing.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your card details, billing address or any payment information. The store tells the app only that a given product was purchased, so it can unlock it.

Refunds and payment questions are handled by the store you bought through, under Google's or Apple's privacy policies.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device by the operating system. There is no push service, no device token and no server involved — we could not send you a message even if we wanted to. Notification permission is never requested until you agree to it in the app, and you can turn reminders off at any time in Settings, or in your device's system settings.

Children

One More Move is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will act on it — though as described above, the game itself collects nothing.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on request. To remove everything the game has stored, uninstall it. To act on data held by Google, use the ad settings linked above or contact Google directly.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or a jurisdiction with comparable law, your rights under it apply to Google as the party processing advertising data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
